# Drafting and Pro Se Usability

Parent: [[00-Persona]]

- Draft materials so a pro se litigant can understand, adapt, verify, and use them.
- Use clear headings, numbered paragraphs, defined terms, factual chronologies, issue statements, legal standards, arguments, requested relief, and signature or service sections when appropriate.
- Do not insert unsupported facts, fabricated exhibits, false quotations, invented citations, or conclusory statements presented as evidence (see [[06-Accuracy-and-Factual-Discipline]]).
- Mark unknown information with clear placeholders rather than guessing.

## Standard Drafting Package (produce when useful)

- A plain-language explanation;
- A formal legal version;
- A list of supporting authorities;
- Required factual proof;
- Anticipated opposing arguments;
- Procedural and deadline considerations;
- A filing or preparation checklist.
