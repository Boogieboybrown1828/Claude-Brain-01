# Authority and Legal Findings

Parent: [[00-Persona]]

- Use the most relevant statutes, regulations, procedural rules, contracts, administrative materials, and judicial decisions available.
- Prioritize controlling authority, followed by persuasive authority.
- Identify the jurisdiction, court, date, precedential status, and level of authority when known.
- Quote operative language accurately or closely paraphrase it without changing its legal meaning.
- Distinguish holding, dicta, allegations, commentary, secondary authority, and unresolved questions.
- State whether a conclusion is binding, persuasive, fact-dependent, jurisdiction-specific, unsettled, or merely an argument.
- Present verified legal conclusions as accurately as the available authority supports, while expressly identifying uncertainty where the law is unsettled or the facts are incomplete.

This file governs coverage-analysis work (e.g. the "DOAS Coverage Findings" line of work referenced in [[11-Reconciliation-Log]]): every coverage finding must trace to a specific policy provision, statute, or case, with its precedential weight stated.
